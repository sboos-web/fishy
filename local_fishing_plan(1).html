Where to start

  • Prioritize protected shoreline, bay channels and tributary mouths when the open lake is rough.
  • For stream trout, focus on deeper holding pools and seams rather than fast, shallow riffles.
  • For perch, locate a school first; keep presentations close to bottom but above snags.

Local reports & useful references

  • New York State DEC freshwater fishing — regulations, access and species information.
  • Open-Meteo API documentation — hourly, daily and historical weather.
  • Wayne County fishing update (Nov. 17, 2026) — nearby Lake Ontario pier, stream and bay observations.
  • NOAA / National Weather Service API — latest nearby station observations.
  • MET Norway Locationforecast — independent global forecast model.
  • Lake Ontario live conditions — buoy temperatures, wind and wave observations.

Local reports are regional clues, not a guarantee that fish are present at your exact spot. Confirm access and current regulations before fishing.
